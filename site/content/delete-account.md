# Delete your Keeploom account

You can delete your account and personal data at any time.

## In the app

- Open Keeploom and sign in.
- Go to Profile, tap the settings button, then tap Delete account.
- Type the confirmation shown on screen and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This can't be undone.

## Without the app

If you can't open the app, email us from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm it's you and delete it within 30 days. {{contact}}

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in details, profile, name, username and profile photo.
- Your group memberships, notifications, settings and device push tokens.
- Groups where you were the only member, with their photos.

## What is kept

- Photos and moments you shared with a group stay with that group for the other members, without your name. Ask the group's owner to delete them first if you want them gone.
- Purchase records that we must keep for accounting and tax, for as long as the law requires.
