# Help & support

Answers to common questions. If you can't find what you need, contact us at the end of this page.

## Groups and invites

- **How do I invite someone?** Open the group, tap the options button at the top and choose Invite. Share the link or the code. A couple group has room for two people.
- **An invite doesn't work.** Invites can expire, be cancelled, or run out of uses. Ask someone in the group for a new one.
- **How do I leave a group?** Open the group, tap the options button, choose Group settings and tap Leave group. If you're the owner, ownership passes to the member who has been there longest.

## Photos

- **Why did an old photo disappear?** A Basic group keeps its latest 10 photos. When a new one is added, the oldest is removed. Keeploom Memories keeps every photo.
- **A photo didn't upload.** Check your connection and tap Retry. Nothing is shared twice.
- **Can I delete a photo?** Yes. Open it, tap the options button and choose Delete photo. You can delete photos you shared; group owners can delete any photo in their group.

## Keeploom Memories

- **What is it?** A yearly pass for one group that keeps every photo for everyone in it.
- **Does it renew automatically?** No. We remind you before it ends, and anyone in the group can renew. A new year is added to the end of the current one.
- **I bought it but the group didn't change.** Open the group's Keeploom Memories page and tap Restore purchases.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple or Google. Request one from the App Store or Google Play.

## Widget and notifications

- **How do I add the widget?** Long-press your home screen, choose Widgets, find Keeploom and pick a group.
- **I'm not getting notifications.** Check that notifications are on in Profile and in your phone's settings for Keeploom.

## Your account

- **I forgot my password.** On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and follow the email.
- **How do I delete my account?** Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. This can't be undone.
