# Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Keeploom is a private place to share photos and moments with the people closest to you: a partner, friends or family. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language. If anything is unclear, contact us (see the end of this page).

## In short

- Your groups are private. Only the members of a group can see its photos, captions, journal and milestones.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your photos to train anything.
- Notifications never include your photos or private captions.
- You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash), your display name, and optionally a username and a profile photo.
- **What you share:** photos, captions, the date a photo was taken, journal entries, milestones and special moments, and the groups you create or join.
- **Purchases:** which group a Keeploom Memories pass applies to, when it was bought and when it ends. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
- **Device details for notifications:** a push token for your phone, so we can tell you when something new is shared. You can turn notifications off in Profile.
- **Basic technical data:** our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and request times) for security and to keep the service running.

We remove location data (GPS) and other hidden metadata from your photos on your phone before they are uploaded.

## How we use it

- To run Keeploom: store your photos and share them with the members of your groups.
- To show your groups on your home screen widget.
- To send notifications you asked for, such as a new photo or an upcoming milestone.
- To check your purchases and unlock Keeploom Memories for the right group.
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell it.

## Who can see what

- **Members of a group** can see everything shared in that group, and the display name, username and profile photo of the other members.
- **Invitations:** anyone you send an invite link or code to can use it to join that group until it expires or is used.
- **Nobody else.** Photos are kept in private storage and shown only through short-lived links to signed-in members.

## Services we use

We use a small number of trusted providers to run Keeploom. They process data only on our behalf:

- **Supabase:** account sign-in, database and private photo storage.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service:** delivering notifications.
- **RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google Play:** processing and checking purchases.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. They are required to protect it.

## How long we keep it

- **Basic groups** keep the latest 10 photos. When a new photo is added to a full group, the oldest one is removed permanently, unless it's pinned in a group with Keeploom Memories.
- **Photos you delete** are removed from storage shortly after.
- **When a Keeploom Memories pass ends,** the group keeps every photo for 30 more days. After that, Basic rules apply again the next time a photo is added.
- **When you delete your account,** we delete your profile, sign-in details, memberships, notifications and settings. Photos and moments you shared with a group stay with that group for the other members, without your name. Groups where you were the only member are deleted.

## Your choices and rights

- See and change your name, username and profile photo in Profile.
- Delete photos you shared, leave any group, and turn notifications off.
- Delete your account and personal data at any time in Profile → Settings → Delete account.
- Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how it's used. Contact us to make a request and we'll reply within 30 days.

## Children

Keeploom is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to each group is checked on our servers for every request, not just in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects you.

## Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
